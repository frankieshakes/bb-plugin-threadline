Find your place in a long thread. Threadline pins a compact widget to the
right edge of every conversation — one tick for each question you asked, laid
out like a document minimap.

## What you get

- A **navigator widget** on the right edge of a thread, with a tick per
  question.
- A **hover popover** listing every question's preview text, newest work in
  context. The question you're currently reading is highlighted in both the
  widget and the list.
- **One-click jumps.** Click a tick or a preview and the conversation scrolls
  to the start of that question and flashes it — even when the message is far
  off-screen in a very long thread.

## How it works

The widget reads the thread's own conversation outline from the BB server, so
previews always match what was actually asked. Navigation drives BB's real
timeline: nothing is duplicated, and nothing leaves your machine.

The widget appears only inside a thread that has at least one question, and
stays out of the way everywhere else. A **Widget position** setting places it at
the top, middle, or bottom (just above the chat input) of the conversation.
