Find your place in a long thread. Threadline lays a slim rail down the right
edge of every conversation — one marker for each prompt you sent, arranged like
a document minimap.

## What you get

- A **prompt rail** on the right edge of a thread, with one right-aligned marker
  per prompt. The prompt you're currently reading stays highlighted as you
  scroll.
- A **hover popover** listing every prompt's preview text, so you can scan the
  whole conversation at a glance and spot the one you want.
- **One-click jumps.** Click a marker or a preview and the conversation scrolls
  to the start of that prompt and flashes it — even when the message is far
  off-screen in a very long thread.
- A **position setting** that places the rail at the top, middle, or bottom
  (just above the chat input) of the conversation.

## How it works

The rail reads the thread's own conversation outline from the BB server, so the
previews always match what you actually sent. Jumps drive BB's real timeline —
including its virtualized, off-screen messages — so nothing is duplicated and
nothing leaves your machine.

## Requirements

Runs entirely inside BB. No account, external service, or separate install. The
rail appears only inside a thread that has at least one prompt, and stays out of
the way everywhere else.
